STAFF-ONLY PROCEDURE — SAMPLE HANDLING AND BIOSAFETY (FICTITIOUS DEMO DOCUMENT, REF DEMO-BIO-07)
LabAssist demonstration laboratory. This document is fictitious and must only be used to test the application.

1. Scope
This procedure applies to all technicians handling primary blood and urine samples in the pre-analytical area.

2. Personal protective equipment
Wear nitrile gloves, a closed lab coat and safety glasses for every manipulation of open tubes. Change gloves between each batch and whenever they are visibly contaminated.

3. Sample reception
Check patient identity on the tube label against the request form using two identifiers (full name and date of birth). Any discrepancy must be recorded as a non-conformity and the sample must not be processed until the identity is confirmed by the collecting site.

4. Centrifugation
Balance the centrifuge rotor before every run. Do not open the centrifuge lid until the rotor has completely stopped. If a tube breaks inside the centrifuge, wait 30 minutes before opening to let aerosols settle, then decontaminate with 0.5% sodium hypochlorite.

5. Spills
For a spill of biological fluid, cover with absorbent paper, pour 0.5% sodium hypochlorite from the outside towards the centre, wait 10 minutes, then collect the waste in the yellow biohazard container.

6. Waste
Sharps go into rigid yellow containers, filled to a maximum of three quarters. Biological waste is collected twice a day by the logistics team.

7. Storage
After analysis, primary samples are kept at 2-8 °C for 7 days in the archive refrigerator (room B12) before disposal.
